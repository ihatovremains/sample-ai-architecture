# Sample AI Architecture

Design studies in vendor-neutral, model-neutral architecture. Each study takes a
problem that is usually answered with a product name, and answers it with
boundaries, cargo rules, and named owners instead.

## Work 01 — [The Boundary Atlas](boundary-atlas/)

Five figures on supply chains that span drifting regulatory regions, and identity
verification that collects nothing it does not need. Borders before arrows; a
ledger someone can sign; a ceremony whose most important arrow is the one that is
not drawn; a deployment that names the single layer where products are allowed to
matter; and a partition drill that treats degradation as a designed outcome.
